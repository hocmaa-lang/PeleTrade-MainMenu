The September email plan

Eight emails to the people who entered the Lavazza Blue giveaway in July through the Facebook ad, and who have heard nothing since the middle of August. The series is built on the four September dates that give an American coffee buyer a real reason to open an email, and on the live catalogue — every product name, price and best-before date on this page was pulled from the store on 2 September, not from an old list.

    What we need before anything sends

    Four things, and all four are on your side. The first is the reason the series opens the way it does.

    01

    Who won the giveaway?

    A winner announcement was never sent to the entrants. The first email needs a first name and a state. If the draw itself has not been made yet, it needs making now — these people have been waiting since the middle of August.

    02

    Two Shopify discount codes

    One entrant code at 10%, live 3–13 September. A second at 20%, live 29 September to 1 October. Both on bestcoffeeandtea.com, both with free shipping over $75.

    03

    A business postal address for the email footer

    Required by the US CAN-SPAM Act. The field currently renders blank.

    04

    Pull the expired stock off the sale collection

    Senseo Strong and Extra Strong (15 Aug), Douwe Egberts Aroma Rood (15 Aug), Mighty Leaf Earl Grey (21 Aug) and Lavazza Expert Decaf (30 Aug) are all past their printed best-before date and still listed. The 6 September email points straight at that shelf.
